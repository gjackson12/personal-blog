The train has left the station and there is no stopping it or you might say the genie is out of the bag. Software engineering will be the same witht he advent of the new frontier models and as much as any of us fear it, we have no choice at this point but to embrace the uncertainity headed our way.

Let me make one thing clear, I welcome these new models and a new tool-set to applly to problems that took far too long. My uneasiness stems from putting in years of work refining my ability to write and read code to provude enterprise systems. and now that "craft" is changing. Of course there will always be engineering problems to solve even with AI as a co-pilot but the adventure of developing a platform from scratch with look far different now.

I remmember starting my career in the "wonderful" world of government consulting working as a data analyst and implementing BI analytics tools. It took months upon months in those days to prepare the data to properlly use in it conjunction with one of the main enterprise tools on the market ike Cognos, or MicroStrategy. Now days I have no doubt you transform the data in a matter of days with the help of our new pal AI.
